DISCRIPTION 

Enhance your interior with this elegant 5 Light Black Wall Sconce, featuring a beautifully curved linear frame, five white fabric lampshades, and a sophisticated dark metal finish with warm antique brass accents. The symmetrical design creates a refined balance between classic elegance and contemporary style, making this long wall light an attractive focal point for your home. Its graceful curved arms and soft white shades complement modern, traditional, transitional, and luxury interiors, adding timeless character to your wall décor.

This five-light wall lamp is ideal for spacious bedrooms, living rooms, dining areas, hallways, entryways, bathroom vanity areas, boutique hotels, and upscale hospitality interiors. Install it above a console table, along a large hallway wall, or in a suitable vanity area to create an elegant lighting feature. The elongated silhouette works especially well on wider walls, while the combination of dark metalwork and white shades coordinates beautifully with neutral walls, marble surfaces, wood furniture, and sophisticated interior finishes.
